MEET JOHN

A COMMITTED VOICE FOR WARD ONE.

John Lorenzini is running for Town of Wappinger Ward One Councilman with a commitment to thoughtful leadership and a strong future for the community.

John believes local government should be responsible, accessible, and focused on the issues that directly affect residents. His campaign is centered on preserving the character of Wappinger while making practical decisions that support public safety, fiscal responsibility, and the community's quality of life.

As your councilman, John will work to ensure that the voices of Ward One residents are heard and that the Town's future is guided by careful planning and responsible stewardship.
